# AigleOn Labs — Services & Systems

AigleOn Labs offers detailed brand strategy and custom UI design through to high-performance development and custom Agentic AI workflows.

## Core services

1. **Brand Audit and Custom Strategy** — a detailed audit of the brand (an extension of a generic audit) followed by a custom strategy built from the audit's metrics.
2. **UI Designs for brand sites** — pixel-perfect visual designs crafted around the brand's colours, typography, palette, and identity.
3. **UX & Conversion Strategy** — conversion-focused layouts and user flows refined through real-user testing so every interaction feels intuitive and drives results.
4. **SEO-First Development** — the finalized design built with clean, semantic code that Google crawlers and AI search engines can easily understand.
5. **GEO and AEO** (Generative Engine Optimisation / Answer Engine Optimisation) — implemented on a specific long-term contract basis, or when requested by the brand.
6. **FAQ Bot & WhatsApp Agent** — a smart FAQ chatbot and WhatsApp agent that handles customer queries instantly, 24/7, without manual effort.
7. **Google Business Profile automation** — GBP optimisation and WhatsApp Business number automation, included as a bonus for every brand AigleOn Labs works with.
8. **AI Review Capture System** — captures details from everyone who visits the site, stores them automatically in a CRM, and facilitates seamless review collection.
9. **AI Lead Nurturing System** — automatically follows up with old leads, re-engages cold contacts, and automates nurturing workflows to increase client retention.

## Technologies used

Figma, Framer, Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Vercel.

8+ projects delivered, 97% client satisfaction, 100% responsive designs.

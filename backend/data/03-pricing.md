# AigleOn Labs — Pricing & Packages

Three service tiers, sized to fit different growth stages. A 12% advance is required to initiate a project on any tier.

## STARTER — One-Time Project ("Visibility Foundation")
Perfect for local brands needing a high-end conversion engine to capture first-time customers.
- **Price:** ₹15,000 – ₹19,000 (India) / $250 – $300 (International)
- **Delivery:** 3 to 4 weeks
- Free brand visibility audit (AEO gap, speed, Google Business Profile status)
- Brand story architecture & messaging hierarchy
- Conversion-focused landing page design & development
- On-page SEO setup (headings, meta, keywords)
- Google Business Profile optimization
- WhatsApp Business basic auto-reply configuration
- One revision round included

## GROWTH — Monthly Retainer ("Search and Convert") — Most Popular
Designed for growing brands wanting consistent, recurring organic visits and search dominance.
- **Price:** ₹22,000 – ₹27,000/mo (India) / $400 – $450/mo (International)
- **Minimum contract:** 3 months
- Includes everything in STARTER
- AEO content structuring (ChatGPT & Perplexity format)
- Schema markup (Product, Local Business, Reviews)
- Subscription flow UX audit & journey redesign
- Google Shopping feed optimization
- Monthly SEO maintenance & content updates
- Monthly plain-language progress report
- Three revision rounds per month

## FULL SYSTEM — Monthly Retainer ("Discovery to Lead")
The ultimate automated pipeline — hands-free marketing, calling, and lead capturing.
- **Price:** ₹50,000 – ₹70,000/mo (India) / $600 – $850/mo (International)
- **Minimum contract:** 3 months
- Includes everything in GROWTH
- Review generation system & automated request flow
- WhatsApp automation (enquiry, order, reorder nudge)
- AI Lead Qualification Agent (24/7 CRM capture)
- AI Voice Agent receptionist for incoming calls
- Unified analytics dashboard (traffic & leads)
- Monthly strategy & growth sync calls
- Unlimited revisions & support

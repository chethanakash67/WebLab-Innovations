# AigleOn Labs — Story & Team

## Founders
- **Chethan Akash** — Founder, brings the eye for interfaces that move visitors from curious to convinced.
- **Sai Videsh** — Founder, brings the technical side: SEO, AEO, and the systems that decide whether a brand shows up when someone searches or asks an AI for a recommendation.

## Quick facts
- **Founded:** 2026, Bangalore.
- **Core specialities:** Conversion-focused UI/UX, Technical SEO, AEO (AI search visibility).
- **Niche focus:** Specialty food and artisan brands — coffee roasters, chocolatiers, craft beverage makers.
- **Markets served:** India & Global.
- **Mission target:** Educate 100+ businesses on digital visibility in the first 3 months.

## Philosophy
AigleOn Labs believes in showing, not telling. Before pitching any service, the team runs a free audit and a live demo on the call, showing exactly where a brand stands today on Google and on AI search engines like ChatGPT — so a founder can see the gap with their own eyes rather than take AigleOn Labs' word for it.

## The story
The two founders met while at IIIT and SRM respectively, both looking to build with what they actually knew rather than follow a conventional resume path. Months of shipping small apps and software followed, without a fixed target market, until they noticed specialty food and artisan brands — coffee roasters, chocolate makers — kept being invisible online not because they were bad businesses, but because nobody had explained why a good-looking website still doesn't get found on Google or recommended by AI. That gap, between having a product worth buying and being findable at all, is where AigleOn Labs was founded. The mission: reach over 100 businesses, show them what's actually happening behind their website, and prove that looking fine and converting a visitor into a customer are not the same thing.

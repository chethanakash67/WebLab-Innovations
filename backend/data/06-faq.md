# AigleOn Labs — Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does it take to set up an AI system for a business (e.g. a cafe)?**
Most systems are fully functional within 5 to 7 days. AigleOn Labs starts by analyzing the business workflow, designs the custom AI logic, and tests it with staff before going live — including API integrations and prompt templates optimized for the specific use case.

**Do I need a developer to run these tools?**
No. AigleOn Labs handles the entire setup, coding, configuration, and integration. Once live, the system runs automatically, and updates can be made through a simple admin dashboard or direct support — no code or hosting management required from the client.

**What's the difference between a loyalty program and a review follow-up system?**
Review follow-up builds public reputation by asking happy customers to post reviews on Google Maps, helping win new customers searching for local options. Loyalty automation keeps existing customers coming back with personalized offers, birthday discounts, and reminders — boosting repeat visits and customer lifetime value.

**How much do these systems cost?**
Pricing depends on business size, customer volume, and the specific automation systems selected. Most tools are designed to pay for themselves quickly by increasing repeat visits and reducing manual work, with clear ROI typically within the first month. Both flat project setups and recurring maintenance plans are offered (see the Pricing document for exact tiers).

**Can it integrate with existing billing or POS software?**
Yes. The systems can connect with modern POS and billing tools through webhook integrations or custom API connections, so the AI knows when to send the right follow-up at the right time. Supported platforms include Shopify, Stripe, Square, Clover, and other major providers.

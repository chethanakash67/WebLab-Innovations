# AigleOn Labs — Overview

AigleOn Labs is a digital systems agency and venture lab. The "Lab" is where AigleOn Labs prototypes the automations, AI agents, and intelligence that power its clients' growth long after a website ships — the always-on layer behind the brand.

## What the Lab builds

- **AI Review Funnels** — automated flows that nudge happy customers to post reviews on Google Maps, lifting local reputation without manual chasing.
- **Lead Capture Agents** — conversational agents that qualify visitors in real time, answer questions instantly, and hand warm leads straight to the business's inbox.
- **Workflow Automation** — wiring a business's POS, billing, and follow-up emails together so repetitive back-office work runs itself in the background.
- **GEO & AEO Systems** — Generative Engine Optimization and Answer Engine Optimization: entity maps and structured data that make LLMs like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Siri recommend the brand when customers ask.

## Track record

- Always-on agents handle enquiries 24/7.
- 8+ automation blueprints ready to deploy.
- Under 1 second response time for AI lead capture.

## Getting in touch

Businesses that want an AI system built for them can reach out via WhatsApp directly from the Lab page, or through the site's contact/audit forms. AigleOn Labs maps an automation to whatever repetitive task is taking up the business owner's time.
